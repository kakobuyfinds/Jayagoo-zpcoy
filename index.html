02 / Jayagoo shopping guide

A better shortlist
in four steps.

Useful Jayagoo finds begin with a clear comparison process. Save the details that help you make a decision rather than collecting links without context.

Find your starting point on CAFANS ↗

Choose a category and a purpose.

Decide whether you need daily sneakers, a heavier outer layer, a matching set or a finishing accessory. Keep products with different uses in separate groups.

Record the exact product information.

Save the listing URL, seller, selected variation, listed currency and the date you checked it. Jayagoo product links need context to remain useful later.

Check fit and supporting photos.

Compare measurements with something you already own. Where available, inspect close-up photos of seams, soles, labels, hardware and the selected variation.

Compare the complete estimated cost.

Keep the item price separate from delivery, service charges, currency conversion and applicable import costs. Confirm current terms before ordering.

Practical answers about this guide, product comparisons and the links you will find here.

What is a Jayagoo spreadsheet?

In this guide, a Jayagoo spreadsheet means a structured shortlist of fashion product links with comparison notes such as category, size, material, price and photo references. This page is a browsing guide, not a downloadable spreadsheet or an official Jayagoo service.

How do I use the product links on this page?

Choose a category to open its CAFANS listing. Review the individual product page, confirm the selected size and color, and check the seller's current information before deciding whether to buy.

What should I record in a clothing or sneaker spreadsheet?

Record the product URL, seller, category, color, size measurements, material, listed price, currency, available photos and the date checked. Keep shipping and other applicable costs separate from the item price.

Are Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci and Hermès products authenticated here?

No. Brand names are reference topics, and editorial photographs do not verify any linked listing. Check product provenance, seller documentation and any independent authentication evidence separately.

Does a listed price include shipping and fees?

Do not assume that it does. Check whether domestic delivery, international shipping, service charges, currency conversion and applicable import charges are included before comparing total costs.

Is this an official Jayagoo website?

No. This is an independent fashion discovery and comparison guide. Its shopping links lead to CAFANS, and it does not claim affiliation with Jayagoo or the referenced fashion brands.
